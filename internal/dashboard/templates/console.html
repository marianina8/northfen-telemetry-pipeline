What you'll see

  1. Readings stream in tick by tick, tagged with your sandbox's session.
  2. For each metric, the first 20 readings learn that pool's normal (mean and noise). After that, every reading gets a z-score and every 10-reading window is scored and stored. This is plain statistics, with no model involved.
  3. When a window crosses a threshold, the pool's metrics are grouped into one alert. After a short settle, one bounded model call explains the likely cause across all the pool's metrics and its recent changes.
  4. A fixed table turns severity and confidence into log, ticket or page. Explanations the model isn't sure about go to a human. You can acknowledge, dismiss or escalate.
